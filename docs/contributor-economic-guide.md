# Contributor Economic Guide

## Purpose

This guide explains how work can become an inspectable economic claim inside the Technocommunistic COBOL Terminal research model.

The objective is simple:

work -> evidence -> measured contribution -> economic claim -> allocation

The system explains economic contribution. It does not assign human worth.

## What Is Separate

Capital != Labor != Contribution != Stewardship

These are different economic claim classes.

Labor is contractual compensation and is accounted for before distributable surplus.

Contribution represents evidence-backed creation of durable capability.

Stewardship represents responsibility for preserving valuable systems through time.

Capital represents a bounded financial claim.

The same person may hold several claim types, but the claims remain separate.

## How Contribution Is Traced

A contribution should be traceable through a value graph:

Contributor
-> Work
-> Artifact
-> Capability
-> Organizational Improvement
-> Requirement / Outcome / Durable Value

A claim should not depend on status, title, visibility, or raw activity volume.

It should depend on inspectable evidence of what was created, what became possible because of it, and how durable or economically relevant that capability is.

## Measurement

No scored parameter without a measurement function.

No measurement without observable evidence.

The intended chain is:

Evidence
-> Observables
-> Measurement Function
-> Normalized Parameter
-> Claim
-> Allocation
-> Money

A contributor should be able to ask what evidence affected a claim and receive a reproducible answer.

## What Does Not Count By Itself

The following must not create economic value merely by existing in large quantities:

- commit count
- ticket count
- message count
- event count
- duplicate dependency records
- repeated labels
- self-declared ownership
- management preference

Activity can produce evidence.

Activity volume alone is not evidence of durable contribution.

## Durable Contribution

The research model is interested in work that creates or improves durable capability.

Examples may include:

- a reusable platform capability
- an integration that enables future business
- infrastructure that removes repeated operational cost
- architecture that reduces systemic fragility
- tooling that permanently improves delivery
- evidence and provenance systems that make decisions reproducible
- commercial work that directly causes measurable economic outcomes

The exact measurement functions remain experimental and must survive adversarial testing.

## Stewardship

Creating valuable capability and maintaining valuable capability are separate economic paths.

A contributor may receive a contribution claim for creating a system.

The same contributor, or another person, may receive a stewardship claim for carrying responsibility for keeping that system operational, safe, maintainable, or available.

The two claims may terminate at the same person.

They must never become the same claim.

## Distribution

The economic flow is:

Revenue
-> Costs
-> Labor
-> Reserve / Reinvestment
-> Distributable Surplus
-> Economic Pools
-> Evidence-backed Claims
-> Deterministic Allocation

Contribution claims compete only inside the Contribution pool.

Stewardship claims compete only inside the Stewardship pool.

Capital claims belong to the Capital pool.

A pool is an economic boundary, not a permission to silently move value between claim classes.

## Understanding Your Result

The intended terminal interface should let a contributor ask:

WHY THIS AMOUNT?

The answer should trace backward through:

allocated amount
-> pool share
-> normalized claim
-> measured parameters
-> observables
-> evidence
-> artifacts
-> dependencies
-> outcomes

A result that cannot be explained through this chain is not sufficiently inspectable.

## Challenging a Result

A contributor should challenge facts and evidence rather than argue about personal worth.

Examples:

"This dependency count is wrong."

"This artifact was attributed to the wrong contributor."

"This capability existed before the claimed work."

"These two dependency paths share the same bottleneck."

"This evidence was duplicated."

Correct the evidence, rerun the deterministic protocol, and inspect the new result.

## Protocol Versions

Economic rules are versioned.

Historical allocations should remain reproducible from:

Evidence + Measurement Rules + Protocol Version

Rules must not be silently rewritten after the economic event they govern.

New research findings may change future protocol versions.

They must not erase the logic of historical ones.

## What Contributors Should Optimize For

The system should encourage work that produces durable, inspectable economic value.

That generally means improving:

- quality
- durability
- systemic usefulness
- real dependency relevance
- reproducibility
- operational resilience
- measurable outcomes
- evidence completeness

The model should actively resist incentives for superficial activity farming.

## Current Research Status

This is experimental research software.

Current formulas and weights are hypotheses, not employment policy, payroll rules, or guaranteed compensation.

Some pathological tests intentionally fail because their purpose is to expose weak economic semantics.

The research standard is simple:

If the implementation disproves the theory, the theory changes.
